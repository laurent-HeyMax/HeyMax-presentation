Photographs are resized to a maximum width of 1800px for the local app. Originals remain in Figma. Artwork is not reconstructed or recoloured. These are supplied brand-reference assets; maintain their internal-use context and confirm usage rights when taking work outside the organisation.

The Plus Jakarta Sans variable WOFF2 was reused from the user’s existing `heymax-design-tools V2` font assets. The bundled font is used across the studio and embedded in exports. Its SIL Open Font License is included at `public/assets/OFL-PlusJakartaSans.txt`, retrieved from the Google Fonts upstream repository. Preserve that licence when distributing the project.

Palette: primary #21005E, secondary #F3EAFF, tertiary #4000B8. Neutrals and accents are derived from the external guideline’s Typography & Colour section (`13:2852`).

Voice and imagery references: `417:16882` (messaging), `2:3550` (photography). Logo usage reference: `16:1788`.

No temporary Figma URLs are used at runtime. No campaign claims or performance numbers from the example decks have been treated as current approved facts.
